Le Morne Brabant

Le Morne Brabant is a majestic basaltic monolith rising 556 meters above sea level on the southwestern tip of Mauritius. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008, this iconic mountain holds deep historical significance as a symbol of freedom and resistance. During the 18th and early 19th centuries, escaped slaves known as maroons sought refuge in the mountain's hidden caves and dense forests, forming isolated communities. Today, Le Morne stands not only as a natural wonder but also as a powerful memorial to the struggle against slavery.
