Casela Nature Park

Casela Nature Park is one of Mauritius' premier adventure and wildlife destinations, nestled at the foothills of the majestic Black River mountain range. Spanning over 14 hectares, this multi-activity park offers a unique blend of wildlife encounters, thrilling adventures, and family-friendly attractions. Home to more than 1,500 animals from 140 different species, including lions, zebras, giraffes, and native Mauritian birds, Casela provides immersive experiences like walk-through aviaries, safari tours, and interactive animal encounters.
