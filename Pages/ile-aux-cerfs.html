About Ile aux Cerfs

Ile aux Cerfs is a stunning privately-owned island located off the east coast of Mauritius, renowned for its pristine white sandy beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and lush tropical vegetation. The island spans approximately 87 hectares and offers a perfect day-trip destination for relaxation, water sports, and natural beauty. Originally named after the deer that once inhabited the island, Ile aux Cerfs is now famous for its world-class golf course designed by Bernhard Langer, which features spectacular ocean views and challenging holes.
